# Claude-Philanthropy-Skill-Library

Great ideas need a home. The goal of this repo is to collect and share a trustworthy source for Claude Skills that are relevant and beneficial for Foundations and other Philanthropic and Grantmaking social impact organizations. Collaborators include Chantal (Coco) Forster, Sarah Small, Hannah Kahn, Samantha Shain.

# Purpose
1. Advance and upskill the field through sharing and collaboration
1. Leverage collective effort and avoid duplication of effort 
1. Improve quality of AI usage and output by making quality resources available and giving/receiving quality feedback
1. Expand what we believe is possible

# Governance structure
As of Aug 2026, decisions can be made by agreement of any 3 of 4 founding collaborators.

Intention to experiment freely, externally share generously and transparently, and not let "perfect" get in the way of "good."

# Skill Skeleton
Propose a new skill on the Issues tab.
Use the template file stored in /Utils and /Skills to get started.
